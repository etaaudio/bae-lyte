# BAE privacy policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

BAE (B-audio Enhancer) is a browser extension published by ETA Audio LLC.

## What BAE collects

Nothing. BAE does not collect, store, transmit or sell any personal information or
browsing data.

## What BAE stores

Your own settings (codec choice, Purist and normalization options, playback speed,
pitch correction, crossfeed, B-Mode, clip guard, output device and display options) are saved in your
browser's local extension storage so they persist
between sessions. They stay on your device. Removing the extension removes them.

## Network requests

BAE makes no network requests. It has no analytics, no advertising and no third-party
code.

## Sites it runs on

BAE Lyte runs on every website, so Purist works in any tab that plays audio or video. On
each page it reads which audio and video formats the player uses and holds its volume at
full level. It does not read
any other page content.
