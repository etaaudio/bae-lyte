# BAE Lyte privacy policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

BAE Lyte is a browser extension published by ETA Audio LLC.

## What BAE Lyte collects

Nothing. BAE Lyte does not collect, store, transmit or sell any personal information or
browsing data.

## What BAE Lyte stores

Your own settings (Purist on or off, and your audio and video codec choice) are saved in
your browser's local extension storage so they persist between sessions. They stay on
your device. Removing the extension removes them.

## Network requests

BAE Lyte sends nothing anywhere. It has no analytics, no advertising and no third-party
code.

To show the sample rate and bit depth of what is playing, BAE Lyte may request the first
part of the audio file or stream playlist the page is already playing, from the same
address the page uses. Nothing about you is added to that request, and what comes back
is only read for its format and then discarded.

## Sites it runs on

BAE Lyte runs on every website, so its settings work in any tab that plays audio or
video. On each page it reads which audio and video formats the player asks for and uses,
and holds its volume at full level when Purist is on. It does not read any other page
content.
